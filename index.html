PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 16 October 2026

FMCS737 (the “app”) is published by ADDIE OÜ, Estonia (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

NO DATA COLLECTED

The app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it makes no network connections of its own: all functions work offline on your device.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

  • The airplane you select (737 Classic, NG or MAX) is saved on your device so that it is pre-marked at the next start.
  • Routes, weights, winds and other data you type into the trainer are held in memory only while the app runs.
  • The navigation database is bundled with the app and read-only. Nothing is added to it, and nothing is downloaded or updated over a network.

Deleting the app removes this information from your device.

PURCHASE AND PAYMENT

The app is distributed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information. The app itself contains no in-app purchases and no subscriptions, and it does not check any purchase or entitlement while it runs. Apple’s handling of purchase data is described in Apple’s own privacy policy.

LINKS

Links on this page that lead to other websites open outside the app; those websites have their own privacy practices.

CHILDREN

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

YOUR RIGHTS AND CONTACT

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For any privacy question, or to exercise your rights under the EU General Data Protection Regulation, contact ADDIE OÜ at info@addieproject.eu. You may also complain to the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon).

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date.

The app is an independent product and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or licensed by any aircraft or equipment manufacturer; the airplane type is named only to describe the interface being trained.
